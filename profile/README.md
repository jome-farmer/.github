# JoME: smart irrigation with a farmer's brain

JoME helps farmers, greenhouse growers and home gardeners water at the right time and in the right amount. It watches the soil, the water and the weather, and it looks after crop health, so the plants get what they need and the water isn't wasted.

## How it works

| Part | What it does | Repo |
|------|--------------|------|
| 🌱 **Controller board** | An ESP32 board in the field that reads the sensors and runs the valves | `SHamBE` |
| 📱 **Mobile app** | Monitor moisture, set schedules and control the system from anywhere (iOS and Android) | `JoME` |
| ☁️ **Backend** | Connects boards and apps, stores the data, and runs the automation | `DouSHamBE` |
| 🩺 **Plant doctor** | An AI agent trained on specialist plant data that spots problems early and recommends treatment | in progress |

## What we believe

- **Technology should work alongside the farmer.** The AI carries agricultural expertise, and the farmer's own experience always has the final say.
- **Every drop counts.** Watering decisions come from real soil, water and weather data, not from guessing.
- **Healthy crops come from early attention.** The plant doctor flags issues before they spread.
